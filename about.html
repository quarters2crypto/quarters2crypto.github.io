About

The notebook, explained.

Quarters2Crypto started as a practical crypto field-notes project — wallets, security habits, digital-economy literacy for ordinary people. That work mattered, and some of it is still useful. But the person writing it kept wandering: into AI tooling, into autonomy and computation, into games, into the strange machinery at the edge of ordinary life.

So the site evolved with them. Q2C is now a casual public builder's notebook — a workbench left out in the open. Things being built, things being learned, speculative ideas, creative experiments, and the occasional useful absurdity. Less formal than a company, more honest than a résumé.

The ConcordiaPax relationship

Q2C belongs to the same person as ConcordiaPax, and they share a mission in spirit: making advanced systems more understandable and more humane. The split is one of tone. ConcordiaPax is the serious systems and business work. Q2C is the playground where the ideas get muddy first. Same hands, different gloves.

Who writes it

Q2C is written and built by Flaveon. The primary publishing home is the Substack newsletter — subscribing there is the best way to follow everything, including experiments that never get their own page.

A small disclaimer

Nothing on this site is financial, legal, or tax advice. Experiments are documented for curiosity and education. Where crypto or money is involved, verify everything yourself and never risk what you cannot afford to lose.
